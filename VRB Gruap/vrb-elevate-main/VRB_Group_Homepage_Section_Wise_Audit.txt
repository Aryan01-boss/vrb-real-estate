VRB GROUP
HOMEPAGE SECTION-BY-SECTION AUDIT
What is missing • Why it matters • How to redesign it • What business information is needed

REPORT PURPOSE Ye report management/client ko homepage ka visual redesign samjhane ke liye hai. Har section ke liye current state, problem, recommended change, required content/data aur business impact clear kiya gaya hai.

Executive Summary
VRB Group ki homepage par core information maujood hai — projects, development segments, company positioning, numbers, trust statements aur testimonials. Lekin current structure kaafi template-driven hai: multiple sections ek doosre se naturally connected nahi lagte, kuch generic/placeholder content visible hai, aur visitor ko “main kis project me interested hoon → kya proof hai → next action kya hai?” ka clear journey nahi milta.
Homepage ko brochure se conversion-oriented real-estate sales page banana hai.
Har section ka ek defined job hona chahiye: attract, explain, prove, qualify, ya convert.
Numbers sirf wahi use hon jo internal records se verify ho sakein; unhe design ke saath source/date ke context me dikhaya ja sakta hai.
Project cards me location, approval/RERA status, project scale, key amenities aur CTA ko standardized format me dikhana chahiye.
Generic template sections (Lorem ipsum, unrelated agents/international cities, generic service labels) ko remove/replace karna chahiye.

1. Header / Navigation
PRIORITY: P1

A. Abhi kya hai
Current header me logo, About VRB, Project, VRB Foundation, EWS/LIG, Media, Blogs aur Contact Us navigation hai.
B. Main problem / kya kami hai
Navigation me primary sales journey clearly prioritized nahi hai.
Project menu broad hai, lekin visitor ko Ongoing / Completed / Jaipur / Kota jaise buying-oriented paths clearly guide kiye ja sakte hain.
Header ka visual treatment premium real-estate positioning ko strongly communicate nahi karta.
Contact CTA hai, lekin site visit, brochure, callback jaise intents separate nahi hain.
C. Isko kaise improve karna hai
Navigation ko Projects, About, Why VRB, Media/Insights aur Contact jaise high-value items tak simplify karein.
Projects ke andar Jaipur/Kota + Ongoing/Completed ko clear hierarchy dein.
Header ko sticky banayein; scroll par compact version + persistent enquiry CTA rakhein.
Primary CTA: Book a Site Visit ya Explore Projects; secondary CTA: Talk to Advisor.
D. Recommended section structure
Logo → Projects → Why VRB → About → Media/Insights → Contact.
Right side: Book Site Visit / Talk to Advisor.
Mobile: hamburger + visible CTA.
E. Business impact
Clear navigation reduces friction and gives high-intent visitors a direct path to projects and enquiries.
CLIENT KO SIMPLE LANGUAGE ME KAISE SAMJHAYE “Header / Navigation me abhi information hai, lekin presentation aur conversion logic weak hai. Hum is section ko sirf beautiful nahi banayenge; iska kaam clear karenge — visitor ko next step tak le jaana.”

2. Hero Section
PRIORITY: P1

A. Abhi kya hai
Hero currently says “We are Redefining Modern Living” with supporting text and a “Discover more” CTA over a large visual.
B. Main problem / kya kami hai
Headline brand-level hai, but visitor ko immediately nahi batata ki VRB exactly kya develop karta hai aur kis geography me.
Hero me hard proof absent hai: years, projects delivered, plots sold, locations, approvals etc.
CTA generic “Discover more” hai.
Image attractive hai, lekin actual VRB project/location ki authenticity stronger ho sakti hai.
C. Isko kaise improve karna hai
Headline ko outcome + category based banayein, e.g. “Planned Townships & Real Estate Developments Built for Long-Term Value.”
Subheading me geography + project categories + trust signal add karein.
2 CTAs: Explore Ongoing Projects and Book a Site Visit.
Hero ke bottom/side me 3–4 verified proof metrics add karein.
Actual project photography / aerial / masterplan imagery ko preference dein.
D. Recommended section structure
Eyebrow: VRB GROUP • REAL ESTATE DEVELOPMENT
Headline: clear category + value proposition.
Subtext: geography + development type.
CTA 1: Explore Projects | CTA 2: Book Site Visit.
Proof strip: verified numbers only.
E. Business impact
Hero is the first sales moment. Visitor ko seconds me samajh aana chahiye ki VRB kya karta hai, kahan karta hai, aur next step kya hai.
CLIENT KO SIMPLE LANGUAGE ME KAISE SAMJHAYE “Hero Section me abhi information hai, lekin presentation aur conversion logic weak hai. Hum is section ko sirf beautiful nahi banayenge; iska kaam clear karenge — visitor ko next step tak le jaana.”

6. Business Numbers / Proof Metrics
PRIORITY: P1

A. Abhi kya hai
Visible screenshot me 10K+ plots sold, 50+ colonies, 12K+ happy clients aur 3,500+ associates dikhte hain.
B. Main problem / kya kami hai
Numbers powerful hain, but definition/time period/verification context nahi diya gaya.
Public page extraction me counters ke underlying text me zero-style placeholder values bhi expose ho rahe hain; animation fail hone par data-quality risk ho sakta hai.
Sales, colonies, clients and associates different definitions hain; user ko counting basis nahi pata.
C. Isko kaise improve karna hai
Dedicated “VRB by the Numbers” proof block banayein.
Updated: Month Year / source note where appropriate.
Only internally verified figures publish karein.
Define every metric precisely.
Unverified number ko qualitative proof statement se replace karein.
D. Recommended section structure
Metric
Verified value
Definition
As-of date
Optional proof/source
E. Business impact
Numbers trust build karte hain, but only when precise, current and defensible.
